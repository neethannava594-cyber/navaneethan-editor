# 🔑 GET YOUR SUPABASE KEYS

## Step-by-Step Guide to Find Your Keys

### Go to Supabase Dashboard

1. **Open:** https://app.supabase.com
2. **Login** with your account
3. **Select your project** (navaneethan-editor)

---

## Find Your Supabase URL

**In your project dashboard:**

1. Click **Settings** (bottom left sidebar)
2. Click **API** (top menu)
3. Look for: **Project URL**
4. **Copy it** - looks like:
   ```
   https://xxxxxxxxxxxxx.supabase.co
   ```

**This is your VARIABLE 1 ✅**

---

## Find Your Anon Key

**In same Settings → API page:**

1. Look for: **Anon public key**
2. It's a long string starting with `eyJ...`
3. **Copy it** - looks like:
   ```
   eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJpc3MiOiJzdXBhYmFzZSIsInJlZiI6I...
   ```

**This is your VARIABLE 2 ✅**
